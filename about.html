About Us

Built Different.
Built to Deliver.

We're a UAE-based exhibition design and event production studio obsessed with quality, deadlines, and making brands impossible to ignore on the show floor.

Our Story

From Renders to Reality, Without Compromise

The Butter Duck was founded on a simple frustration: too many exhibition companies over-promise on the render and under-deliver on the floor. We decided to build a studio where the final product matches — or exceeds — the 3D visual. Every time.

Today, we operate from the UAE and deploy globally. Our team combines architectural design, structural engineering, AV expertise, and project management into a single integrated pipeline. No middlemen. No surprises.
