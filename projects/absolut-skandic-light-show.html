Premium Lighting Brand
Light Show 2025

36 sqm • Dubai, UAE

Overview

The Project

A 36 sqm custom-built stand for the client at Light Show 2025, designed to function as a fully immersive lighting showroom on the exhibition floor. The stand combined warm natural materials with precision-engineered product displays, giving buyers a real-world sense of how each fixture performs in architectural settings.

The Challenge

What We Were Up Against

01

Creating a showroom-quality lighting experience within a compact 36 sqm trade show footprint — where ambient hall lighting and neighbouring stands constantly compete for attention.

02

Every fixture needed to be seen under controlled conditions, not washed out by overhead fluorescents.

Our Solution

How We Made It Happen

01

The design placed the product at the centre of every decision.

02

A full-height wooden slat feature wall with concealed blue LED uplighting served as the visual anchor, drawing visitors from the aisle while demonstrating colour-wash capability in real time.

03

Pendant light displays were suspended at varied heights across the stand, turning the ceiling plane into a working product catalogue.

04

A lounge meeting zone and reception desk with branded signage completed the layout, giving the sales team dedicated spaces for every stage of the conversation — from casual browse to formal specification.

Project Photos

Key Features

Engineering Highlights

Wooden Slat Feature Wall with LED Uplighting

Floor-to-ceiling timber slat wall with concealed blue LED channels — a signature backdrop that demonstrated colour-wash lighting in a real architectural context.

Pendant Light Product Display

Multiple pendant fixtures suspended at staggered heights across the stand, creating a working showroom ceiling that let buyers evaluate each product in situ.

Lounge Meeting Zone

Furnished seating area with a meeting table, positioned away from foot traffic — purpose-built for specification discussions and project briefings.

Reception Desk with Branded Signage

Concrete-effect reception counter with illuminated brand graphics, serving as the first touchpoint and visitor registration hub.

Results

The Outcome

  • Stand footfall exceeded client projections by 35% across both show days
  • 18 specification meetings hosted with architects and lighting consultants
  • Product display format adopted by the client for their permanent showroom refresh
  • Contract renewed for Light Show 2026 within two weeks of event close
